# Four detailed original Panjabi designs: 16–19

Four original Roblox Classic Shirt textures with decoration throughout the garment. One soft-fold PNG per design, retaining visible cloth weave and avoiding glossy reflections.

| Design | Wearable PNG | Decoration beyond collar and cuffs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 Navy–Ivory Ajrak | [PNG](panjabi-16-navy-ivory-ajrak.png) | Navy/ivory geometric star-and-rosette lattice across front, back, side panels and sleeves; matching collar, cuffs and torso hem. |
| 17 Sage Garden | [PNG](panjabi-17-sage-cream-vines.png) | Olive leaf trails and cream buds throughout the sage fabric, with open space between vines; matching leaf borders around cuffs and torso hem. |
| 18 Ochre Paisley | [PNG](panjabi-18-ochre-maroon-paisley.png) | Maroon/cream paisleys and seed motifs throughout ochre cloth, extra vertical front border rails, patterned collar, cuffs and hem. |
| 19 Plum Trellis | [PNG](panjabi-19-plum-rose-trellis.png) | Rose/copper diamond floral trellis throughout plum cloth, a double curved chest frame, coordinated collar, cuffs and hem. |

All four PNGs are 585 x 559, 8-bit RGBA. Their actual alpha channels match the approved Sample03 exactly, preserving the neckline and sleeve/hand openings. The unused canvas is opaque white and carries `2.94`. The torso faces were sampled from a continuous wrap field; decoration is present on the back and side faces as well as the chest. Soft fold shading is applied to the woven pattern; no additional no-fold versions are included.
