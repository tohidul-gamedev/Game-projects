# Five more original layered Panjabi designs: 49–53

## মিডনাইট ও আইভরি কাঁথা শোল্ডার-প্যানেল

[panjabi-49-midnight-ivory-kantha-shoulder-panel.png](panjabi-49-midnight-ivory-kantha-shoulder-panel.png)

Midnight cotton with tiny ivory seeds, one triangular Kantha shoulder cloth, folk rosette, folded pleats, wooden pin and angled fringe. Continues over one shoulder cap and back half.

## চারকোল ও ক্রিমসন টগল জ্যাকেট

[panjabi-50-charcoal-crimson-frog-jacket.png](panjabi-50-charcoal-crimson-frog-jacket.png)

Textured charcoal with rust buti, a long crimson brocade panel, four fabric frog/wood-toggle closures, angled welt pocket and botanical spray. Matching brocade side/back panel and cuffs.

## মিন্ট ও রোজ শাল-কলার কোট

[panjabi-51-mint-rose-shawl-collar-coat.png](panjabi-51-mint-rose-shawl-collar-coat.png)

Mint kurta under an open dusty-rose floral coat, broad rounded shawl lapels, braided fastening loop, curved edges and rounded patch pockets. Floral back and clean mint sleeves.

## অকার ও স্লেট ইউটিলিটি কটি

[panjabi-52-ochre-slate-utility-koti.png](panjabi-52-ochre-slate-utility-koti.png)

Ochre diamond-print kurta under a slate utility koti with two large flap chest pockets, wooden toggles, slanted lower welts, rust binding and Kantha stitch panels. Stitched back yoke; no badges or military insignia.

## আইভরি ও মেরুন কোমরের গামছা

[panjabi-53-ivory-maroon-waist-gamcha.png](panjabi-53-ivory-maroon-waist-gamcha.png)

Ivory botanical blockprint in maroon/charcoal, with a maroon checked waist gamcha, gathered folds, side knot, asymmetric fringed flap and matching checked cuffs. Waist drape continues around back and sides.
